# Rename log: "Version B" -> contemporaneous CITS (2026-10-05)

"Version B" (and "Version B-safe") was an internal development label for CITS with a
contemporaneous PC step, v-structure orientation (no Meek) and a union LSCM refit; in the
package this is `cits.cits_contemporaneous` (v1.9.1; formerly `cits.cits_versionb`).
Computations are unchanged.
